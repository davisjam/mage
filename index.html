Model-Based Agentic Software Engineering

Engineering software when implementation is abundant but judgment remains scarce. Capable agents radically reduce the cost of realizing changes; they do not eliminate the work of determining what should be built, which obligations govern it, how evidence should be read, or whether the result is acceptable.
